I presented at the 20th Annual Conference of the European Academy of Management (EURAM). Originally planned for Trinity Business School in Dublin, the conference was held online from 4 to 6 December 2020 because of the COVID-19 pandemic.

The paper, "Rolling stone gathers no moss: The role of change in the successions of family firms", is co-authored with Jan-Philipp Ahrens, Michael Woywode and Baris Istipliler, and looks at how change shapes succession processes in family firms.
