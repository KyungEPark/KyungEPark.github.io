I presented at the sixth International ZEW Conference on the Dynamics of Entrepreneurship (CoDE), held at ZEW in Mannheim on 9 and 10 October 2025. The paper, "Gender Along the Stages of Social Entrepreneurship", is co-authored with Cesare Riillo and follows how gender differences emerge across the successive stages of social entrepreneurship.
