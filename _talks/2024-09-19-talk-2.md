I presented my working paper at the 22nd Interdisciplinary European Conference on Entrepreneurship Research (IECER) in Innsbruck, Austria. The paper, “Greener Pasture: How Board Gender Quotas Affect Female Entrepreneurship”, extends my earlier work, “Stay for a Better Shot – Impact of Gender Quota on Female Entrepreneurship”, by adding an experiment designed to capture how board gender quotas change the advancement opportunities women perceive, and how that in turn shapes the decision to move into entrepreneurship.
