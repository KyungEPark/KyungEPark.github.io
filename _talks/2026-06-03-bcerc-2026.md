I presented at the 46th Babson College Entrepreneurship Research Conference (BCERC), held at the University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa from 3 to 6 June 2026. The paper, "From Possibility to Plausibility: How Entrepreneurs' Orientation Shapes Artificial Intelligence Use in Venture Ideation", is co-authored with Baris Istipliler, Sondos Abdelgawad and Mujtaba Ahsan.

**The paper received the Best Paper Award at the conference.**

The study asks how entrepreneurs' orientation shapes the way they use artificial intelligence when generating and evaluating venture ideas, and how AI support moves an idea from something merely possible to something an entrepreneur treats as plausible.
