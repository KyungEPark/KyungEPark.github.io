I presented at the 85th Academy of Management Annual Meeting, held in Copenhagen from 25 to 29 July 2025. The paper, "Managers, the Midlife Crisis, and Sustainability", is co-authored with G. F. Manganaro and J. P. Ahrens, and examines how managers' life stage relates to the sustainability choices they make.

It appears in the conference proceedings as Manganaro, G. F., Park, K. E., & Ahrens, J. P. (2025). Managers, the Midlife Crisis, and Sustainability. *Academy of Management Proceedings*, 2025(1), 19589.
